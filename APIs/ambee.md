### Ambee

Environmental-intelligence API from Bengaluru-based Ambee, where weather is one dataset in a wider climate suite spanning air quality, pollen, wildfire, natural disasters, and influenza-like illness. The Weather API itself is a compact current / 48-hour-history / short-range-forecast product at claimed 500 m resolution, sold without any public price list.

- **Based in:** Bengaluru (Bangalore), Karnataka, India — the operating team and founding base (Church Street, Shanthala Nagar); the contracting entity is **Ambee Inc.**, whose registered office is 300 Witherspoon Street, Princeton, New Jersey, USA, with sales offices in Boston and New York. Note the terms are governed by the law of Bangalore with arbitration seated in New Delhi under India's Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 — so the legal centre of gravity is Indian despite the US registration
- **Access model:** Mixed — instant self-serve for the free key (sign up on the API dashboard with a Google or business account; the key appears immediately and can be reset every 30 days), but every paid/Enterprise route is **sales contact**. There is no public pricing page at all — the site's "Pricing" nav link is a dead anchor. Multiple API keys are an Enterprise-only feature
- **Pricing model:** Custom / contact for pricing (free self-serve trial key; no published paid tiers or rates at any level)
- **Coverage / data types:** *Weather* — current conditions, 48-hour history, and short-range forecast by lat/lon (temperature, humidity, cloud cover, precipitation intensity, pressure, dew point, visibility, UV index, wind attributes, plus a summary icon set); metric or SI units. *Rest of the suite, each a separate endpoint family* — air quality (latest/history/forecast, by coordinates, city, country, or postal code), pollen (35+ allergens, 48- and 120-hour forecasts), wildfire (latest global, 4-week risk forecast North America only), natural disasters (latest and history, by coordinates/country/continent/event ID), location services (geocoding and reverse geocoding), elevation (North America only), astronomy, and influenza-like illness (beta, 30-day forecast, 7 countries only). Also map tile layers (AQI, pollen), webhooks and alerts, an MCP server, and a Zapier integration. 37 endpoints in total; response localisation into 8 languages on demand
- **Free tier:** a free API key is issued instantly on signup, but **the quota is not published in the documentation** — and Ambee's own marketing pages disagree with each other, variously citing 100 records/day for 15 days and 1,000 calls/day. The dashboard is the only authoritative source; treat the free allotment as trial-shaped and unverified until checked there
- **Paid tiers:** custom / contact for pricing — no figures published anywhere on the site
- **Rate limits:** not published. Each key carries a rate limit and a daily quota visible only in the API dashboard. The error table documents the mechanics: HTTP 429 for rate limiting, 422 for quota exhaustion, and an unusual **HTTP 206 "partial data"** response that trims the payload to whatever remains of your plan allowance
- **Output formats:** JSON (REST); raster map tiles for the AQI and pollen layers; bulk delivery also offered as CSV, NetCDF, or Parquet over SFTP or S3, and via the Databricks and Datarade marketplaces
- **Geographic strength:** global land coverage — the weather endpoints state "global except the Arctic, Antarctica, and any oceans", with 150+ countries and a claimed 500 m spatial resolution; the company's own origin and densest sensor history is India (its first street-level heatmap was Bengaluru). Several sibling datasets are regionally bounded: pollen excludes South America, wildfire risk and elevation are North America only, and ILI covers seven countries
- **Notable limitations:** the weather forecast horizon is very short and the docs contradict themselves on it — the endpoint table says 48 hours while the prose above it says 72, with anything beyond that routed to sales; API history is likewise only 48 hours (the advertised "30+ years" of history is a separate downloadable product, not the API); there are no severe-weather-alert or marine endpoints in the Weather API (disasters are a separate dataset, and oceans are excluded outright); no pricing is published at any tier and the free quota isn't documented; the licence granted in the terms is worded as "non-commercial... for your personal or internal business purpose", which sits awkwardly with commercial use and is worth clarifying in writing before building on it; the terms also forbid creating derivative works or scraping, claim ownership of any derivative work made without permission, and cap total liability at ₹500; this is an environmental-data platform first, so weather is a thinner product here than at a dedicated forecast provider
- **Built on:** Ambee describes a proprietary pipeline unifying satellite data, its own ground sensor network (originally built for air quality), radar, and climate models into a single analytics-grade layer; it publishes a source-attribution PDF in the docs and is a data partner on NASA's PACE mission, but the underlying NWP models behind the weather forecast are not publicly enumerated
