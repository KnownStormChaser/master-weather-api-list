### Solcast

Solar-first weather API from Sydney-based Solcast (a DNV company), built around satellite cloud-tracking rather than generic NWP output. Irradiance, PV power, and a solid supporting set of weather parameters from -7 days to +14 days ahead, at 5-minute granularity and 90 m spatial resolution, plus historical time series back to 2007 and Typical Meteorological Year files. Every plan is quote-only, but a self-serve toolkit account gives a small free allotment and unlimited requests at eight fixed test locations.

- **Based in:** Sydney, New South Wales, Australia (founded 2016 by meteorologist James Luffman and PV-modelling researcher Dr Nick Engerer); acquired by **DNV** — the Norwegian assurance and risk-management group, headquartered in Høvik, near Oslo — in January 2023, and now operating as "Solcast, a DNV company" with the team retained in Australia and offices across Asia, Europe, and the Americas. Support and sales now route to `@dnv.com` addresses
- **Access model:** Mixed — a **self-serve toolkit account** (register at `toolkit.solcast.com.au`, get an API key immediately) provides a free evaluation allotment; every paid plan across all four products is **"Request a quote"** with no self-serve checkout. Authentication is a Bearer token; an interactive docs console, Python and C# SDKs, and a live OpenAPI spec are all published openly
- **Pricing model:** Custom / contact for pricing (self-serve free evaluation tier; four named plan tiers per product with published *specifications* but no published prices at any level)
- **Coverage / data types:** four irradiance-and-weather products, each sold separately — **Forecast** (now to +14 days), **Live** (-7 days to now), **Historical Time Series** (January 2007 to -7 days), and **Typical Meteorological Year** (2007–2025, P50 on TMY Pro; P50/P75/P90/P95 on TMY Max). *Irradiance* — GHI, DNI, DHI, GTI, with clear-sky and probabilistic (P10/P50/P90) variants. *Solar* — rooftop PV power output, solar zenith/azimuth, daily albedo, snow-soiling loss (rooftop and ground-mounted), cloud opacity. *Weather* — air temperature, wind gust, wind speed and direction at 10 m and 100 m, relative humidity, surface pressure, precipitable water, PM2.5, PM10, precipitation rate, dewpoint, snow depth and snowfall rate, snow depth water equivalent, CAPE, and a weather string. *Soiling* — Kimber and HSU loss models. Separately priced **power forecasting models** cover solar (Rooftop/Advanced/Premium), wind, and aggregated market/portfolio/grid products, and DNV's Solar Resource Compass and SolarFarmer are sold alongside. **No severe-weather alerts, radar or satellite imagery, marine, tide, or air-quality index products** — PM2.5/PM10 are raw parameters, not an AQI service
- **Free tier:** self-serve on registration — up to **10 free Live and Forecast requests** and **15 free Historical Time Series requests** (site-months) at locations of your choice, plus **unlimited requests at eight fixed "unmetered locations"** (Sydney Opera House, Grand Canyon, Stonehenge, the Colosseum, Giza, the Taj Mahal, and the Fort Peck and Goodwin Creek SURFRAD stations), matched to four decimal places. Extended free trials are available on request. Unmetered locations don't apply to the legacy Weather Sites / Utility Scale Sites / Rooftop Sites / World API products
- **Paid tiers:** quote-only, but the tier ladder is published per product. *Forecast and Live* — Starter (updates every 6 hours; 30/60-minute granularity), Pro (every 15 minutes; 15/20/30/60-minute), Max (every 5 minutes; 5/10/15/20/30/60-minute), plus Custom. *Historical Time Series* — Starter (3 years back, 30/60-minute), Pro (7 years back, 5-minute granularity), Max (to January 2007, 5-minute), plus Custom. *TMY* — TMY Pro, TMY Max, Site-Adapted TMY. SLAs, 24/7 support, and the Advanced/Premium PV power models are add-ons. Custom plans cover gridded data, area averages, historical forecasts, bulk API access, and site adaptation
- **Rate limits:** not published — usage is metered as request/transaction counts against a quota rather than a documented per-minute limit; the toolkit dashboard shows remaining transactions. Bulk API access is a Custom-plan option
- **Output formats:** JSON, CSV, and HTML from the API; TMY files additionally export as PVsyst, SAM, and TMY3. Python and C# SDKs are published, and the OpenAPI spec is public
- **Geographic strength:** global, but resolution is tiered — 90 m for irradiance, PV power, snow-soiling and cloud parameters over all non-polar continental areas and nearby islands, dropping to 27 km for other parameters and for polar and remote oceanic regions. Cloud tracking runs at 1–2 km and 5 minutes from geostationary satellite imagery. Australia is the home market, but coverage and validation are genuinely worldwide across 100+ countries
- **Notable limitations:** this is a **solar-first product, not a general-purpose weather API** — the weather parameters are there to support irradiance and PV modelling, and there are no alerts, radar, marine, or forecast-text products, so it complements rather than replaces a conventional forecast provider; no prices are published at any tier and every plan requires a quote, though the plan *specifications* are unusually well documented for a quote-only vendor; the four products (Forecast, Live, Historical, TMY) are separate purchases with multi-plan discounts negotiated, and the PV power models are further add-ons; update frequency and time granularity — arguably the core value of a nowcasting product — are the main things gated by tier, so the cheapest plan updates only every 6 hours; the free allotment is small and evaluation-shaped, with the genuinely unlimited option restricted to eight fixed coordinates; polar and remote ocean coverage is coarse; a legacy response format is being retired on 1 February 2027, so older integrations will need updating
- **Built on:** Solcast's own satellite-derived nowcasting and forecasting pipeline rather than resold NWP — geostationary weather-satellite imagery is used to track and predict cloud movement at 1–2 km and 5-minute resolution, downscaled to 90 m with explicit treatment of aerosol and albedo effects, then fused with mesoscale numerical weather models for the longer forecast horizons. Solcast reports producing 600M+ forecasts per hour and publishes independent accuracy validation, including a DNV bankability report; the specific third-party NWP models in the blend are not publicly enumerated
