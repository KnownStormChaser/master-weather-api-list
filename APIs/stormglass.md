### Stormglass

Swedish marine-first weather API that aggregates a dozen national meteorological institutions and AI models behind a single coordinate-based interface — weather, waves, currents, tides, solar, biological oceanography, and bathymetry — letting you request any named source directly or let Stormglass pick the best-performing one per location.

- **Based in:** Degerhamn, Öland, Kalmar County, Sweden (Storm Glass AB, Swedish company reg. 559263-9917; the service has been "developed in Sweden since 2018"). Terms are governed by Swedish law, with arbitration seated in Stockholm and conducted in Swedish — note also that an unrelated Swedish entity named "Stormglass AB" (reg. 559002-1944, formerly Hulu Rehab AB, in liquidation) exists and can confuse due-diligence searches
- **Access model:** Instant self-serve — register on the dashboard, get an API key immediately; the Free, Small, Medium, and Large plans are all self-serve card subscriptions handled by Stripe. Custom Enterprise plans and invoicing go through sales contact (`account@stormglass.io`, with a stated 24-hour response)
- **Pricing model:** Paid — the €0 plan is contractually an evaluation trial rather than an ongoing free tier (see free tier and limitations); self-serve paid plans above it, Enterprise custom
- **Coverage / data types:** all queries are by coordinate against a set of point endpoints (`/v2/weather/point`, `/v2/bio/point`, `/v2/tide/extremes/point`, `/v2/tide/sea-level/point`, `/v2/solar/point`, `/v2/astronomy/point`, `/v2/elevation/point`). *Weather* — air temperature, pressure, wind, gust, precipitation, humidity, dew point, visibility, snow, soil moisture and soil temperature. *Marine* — wave, swell and secondary swell, wind wave, current, water temperature, sea ice, sea depth. *Biological oceanography* — chlorophyll, salinity, pH, dissolved oxygen, phytoplankton. *Tide* — high/low extremes and sea level, with tide-station name and distance-to-station metadata. *Solar* — radiation, UV, cloud cover. Plus astronomy and elevation/bathymetry. Historical data runs from 1 July 2018 across all sources, with NOAA global history back to 1 January 2017. No severe-weather warnings, radar/satellite imagery, or map-tile products
- **Free tier:** €0/month — 10 requests/day with access to all weather parameters, no support, and **not for commercial use**. The terms state plainly that the free test plan is "intended solely for evaluation of the Service prior to purchasing a paid plan and is not meant for indefinite ongoing use," so this is a trial in substance even though it isn't time-boxed
- **Paid tiers (EUR/month; 10% off billed annually):** Small €19 (500 requests/day, all parameters, no support); Medium €49 (5,000/day, full support, commercial use); Large €129 (25,000/day, full support, commercial use); custom Enterprise plans with invoicing via sales contact
- **Rate limits:** per-plan daily request quotas (10/day free → 25,000/day on Large); no per-minute or per-second limit published on the public pages. Enterprise volumes are set by contract
- **Output formats:** JSON (REST); a single request can return multiple named sources side by side for the same parameter
- **Geographic strength:** global by coordinate, but the depth is marine and Northern European — the source list leans on Nordic and Western European institutes (SMHI, FMI, MET Norway/YR, DWD, FCOO, Météo-France, UK Met Office) alongside the global NOAA and ECMWF models, and the marine, tide, and oceanographic products are the core of the offering rather than add-ons
- **Notable limitations:** the free plan is tiny (10 requests/day), non-commercial, and explicitly evaluation-only per the terms — any real use needs a paid plan; **commercial use is listed as a feature of the €49 Medium tier and above, and the €19 Small tier's description omits it**, so anyone planning commercial use on Small should confirm in writing first; support likewise starts at Medium (Free and Small are explicitly "no support"); pricing is EUR-only; no refunds are given under the terms, and liability is capped at one subscription period's fee; there are no severe-weather-alert, radar, satellite, or map-tile products, and output is JSON-only; the API documentation is a JavaScript-rendered single-page app that blocks automated access, so parameter-level and source-level details can only be read in a browser; terms and privacy policy are combined on one page, and Stormglass is explicit that it does not generate the forecasts and accepts no responsibility for their correctness
- **Built on:** an aggregation layer over third-party models rather than in-house forecasting — disclosed sources include NOAA (GFS and the AIGFS AI model), ECMWF (IFS and the AIFS AI model), DWD ICON, Météo-France, the UK Met Office, SMHI, FMI, MET Norway (YR), and FCOO. The `sg` source is Stormglass's own "intelligent global grid," which automatically selects the best-performing underlying source per location; users can also pin any individual source explicitly and compare raw provider performance in the platform
