### Weathernews (WxTech®︎ Data)

Weather data API from Weathernews Inc. — the Japanese company that bills itself as the world's largest private weather service, running its own 13,000-station observation network plus crowd-sourced user reports. WxTech®︎ Data exposes 1 km-mesh Japanese forecasts, observations, historical data, and a wide set of lifestyle and risk indices, alongside 5 km global forecasts, a separate European forecast product, and aviation-grade high-altitude airspace data. No self-serve signup: every route goes through a sales conversation.

- **Based in:** Chiba City (Mihama-ku, Makuhari Techno Garden), Chiba Prefecture, Japan — Weathernews Inc. (株式会社ウェザーニューズ), founded 1986, listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange (4825); service and operation centres also in Oklahoma, Copenhagen, Amsterdam, Paris, Manila, and Yangon
- **Access model:** Sales contact — no public signup, no self-serve key issuance and no published price list; you submit an inquiry, a quote request, or a free-trial request form and wait for a business conversation. Authentication is by an `X-API-Key` header once issued. One exception: the Pollen Counts open-data endpoint requires no key at all (see free tier)
- **Pricing model:** Custom / contact for pricing (quoted per use case; no public figures at any tier)
- **Coverage / data types:** *Japan (1 km mesh unless noted)* — pinpoint weather & feel-like forecast (hourly to 3 days, daily to 10 days), current weather, 5-minute-interval forecast, snow depth forecast and current snow depth, high-resolution historical weather, rain radar tiles (past hour plus next hour), and municipality-level CSV bulk products; *indices* — power outage risk, PM2.5 (15 km), pollen, yellow dust (kōsa), UV, heat stroke risk (WBGT), and dryness; *global* — 5 km mesh global weather forecast, plus a Remote MCP server (beta) serving 1 km Japan and 5 km global forecasts for AI agents; *Europe* — separate short-range (72 h) and medium-range (14 day) point forecasts at 15/30/60-minute resolution, plus a short-range wind pack by height; *high-altitude airspace* — WAFS turbulence/icing/CB and wind/temp/RH/height grids (0.25° and 1.25°, via WIFS and SADIS from both London EGRR and Washington KWBC), Weathernews' own ASC-scale significant weather data, VAAC volcanic ash advisories in GeoJSON, and SIGMET text
- **Free tier:** none as an ongoing commercial tier — a **free trial** is offered but only via a request form. Separately, the **Pollen Counts** endpoint (`/opendata/v1/pollen`, data from Weathernews' proprietary "Pollen Robo" sensors) is published as genuine open data: free download, no API key, CSV output, results may be publicly displayed or broadcast with attribution to Weathernews' Pollen Robo and no prior notification. Commercial/for-profit use or online republication of that data still requires contacting them, and excessive use may be throttled
- **Paid tiers:** custom / contact for pricing
- **Rate limits:** not published (contract-dependent); the open-data pollen endpoint states only that access may be restricted if excessive usage is detected, and caps each query to a 31-day observation window
- **Output formats:** JSON (REST, `application/json`); CSV for the open pollen data and the municipality bulk products; GeoJSON for volcanic ash advisories; plain text for SIGMET; PNG tile URLs for rain radar
- **Geographic strength:** Japan by a wide margin — 1 km mesh resolution nationwide, built on Japan's largest private observation network (13,000+ locations) supplemented by user-submitted reports. Global forecasts are a coarser 5 km product, Europe is served by a distinct regional product line, and the aviation datasets are global
- **Notable limitations:** no self-serve access at all — there is no way to get a key, see a price, or evaluate the API without contacting sales, which makes it impractical for hobbyists and slow for prototyping; pricing is entirely unpublished; most of the catalog is **Japan-only** (all 1 km mesh products, all seven indices, radar, snow, and historical), with global coverage limited to the 5 km forecast and the aviation feeds; the European products are a separate line with their own endpoints rather than part of one global API; documentation is thorough but weather codes, feel-like index values, and wind directions are all custom enumerations needing lookup tables; the WAFS aviation datasets are redistributed from the World Area Forecast Centres and are described as premium data, so licensing terms likely follow the WAFC/SADIS/WIFS regime rather than Weathernews' own; rate limits and SLA terms are contract-specific and invisible before signing. Japanese customers should also note the Meteorological Service Act compliance angle Weathernews markets around
- **Built on:** Weathernews' own observation and analysis infrastructure — a proprietary network of 13,000+ observation sites (including its Soratena/Pollen Robo IoT sensors and WITH Radar), augmented by crowd-sourced reports from its consumer app userbase and its in-house forecasting operation staffed by several hundred meteorologists. The high-altitude airspace products are redistributed WAFC (EGRR/KWBC) WAFS data alongside Weathernews' proprietary ASC scale. The underlying NWP models are not publicly enumerated
