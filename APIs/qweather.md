### QWeather

Global weather API from Beijing-based QWeather (和风天气), covering 500,000+ cities across China and 200+ countries with real-time conditions, 30-day daily and 168-hour hourly forecasts, warnings, air quality, indices, tides, solar radiation, and historical data. Entirely pay-as-you-go with a genuinely large monthly free allotment, and unusually strong English documentation for a Chinese provider.

- **Based in:** Beijing, China (ICP filing 京ICP备15048401号-11)
- **Access model:** Instant self-serve — register, create a project and credential, and start; each account gets its own dedicated **API Host** subdomain (e.g. `abcxyz.qweatherapi.com`) rather than a shared endpoint. Authentication is by JWT (`Authorization: Bearer`) or API key header (`X-QW-Api-Key`). Enterprise/organization volumes (roughly 1,000,000+ requests/day) go through sales contact
- **Pricing model:** Freemium (ongoing monthly free allotment on the main price group, then tiered pay-as-you-go; optional prepaid Savings Plans; enterprise custom) — note the free allotment does **not** extend to all data services, see limitations
- **Coverage / data types:** real-time weather (15-min updates), daily forecast to 30 days, hourly forecast to 168 hours, plus separate grid-based real-time/daily/hourly products; minutely precipitation nowcast (5-min steps, 1–2 h, **China only**); weather warnings (5-min updates, coverage varies by country); air quality (real-time, 7-day daily, 72-hour hourly); Time Machine historical data; 16 weather indices (5 global — car wash, clothing, exercise, UV, fishing — the other 11 China-only); astronomy (sun/moon rise-set and moon phase, 1–60 days); ocean tides (1–10 days); solar radiation forecast (15-min steps, 1–60 h); tropical cyclone list/forecast/track (**China only**); GeoAPI city lookup. Also iOS and Android SDKs
- **Free tier:** ongoing — the first **50,000 requests/month are free** on the "Weather and Essential Services" price group (Weather, Minutely, Warning, Indices, Air Quality, Time Machine, GeoAPI, Astronomy, Console API). Commercial use is permitted. The tier resets monthly; Storm/Ocean and Solar Radiation have **no** free allotment
- **Paid tiers (CNY, per request, tiered monthly — VAT-inclusive for China customers):** *Weather and Essential Services* — first 50,000 free, next 950,000 at ¥0.0007, next 4M at ¥0.0005, next 5M at ¥0.00035, next 40M at ¥0.00015, next 50M at ¥0.0001, over 100M contact sales. *Storm and Ocean* — first 1M at ¥0.003, next 4M at ¥0.0025, next 5M at ¥0.0015, over 10M contact. *Solar Radiation* — first 100,000 at ¥0.3, next 400,000 at ¥0.2, over 500,000 contact. Savings Plans (prepaid annual commitment) are stated to cut 40–70% off pay-as-you-go
- **Rate limits:** a per-minute cap (QPM) applies and returns HTTP 429; QPM is counted per project, with all keys under a project sharing the allowance, and the figure isn't published on the public docs (it's tied to your plan and shown in the Console). Non-`2xx` responses aren't billed, but sustained error or 429 traffic can suspend an account. Tiered pricing resets to tier 1 monthly; charges accrue hourly
- **Output formats:** JSON (HTTPS only, Gzip-compressed by default — responses must be decompressed); iOS and Android SDKs
- **Geographic strength:** global (200+ countries, 500,000+ cities), but strongest in China, Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan — the China-only products (minutely precipitation, tropical cyclone, and 11 of the 16 indices) are where the depth is. Global delivery is via QWeather's own data centres and CDN edge acceleration
- **Notable limitations:** attribution is **mandatory** regardless of plan — products must credit QWeather and name certain data sources, and failure to do so can end access; GeoAPI data may **not** be cached, bulk-downloaded, or indexed for copyright reasons (all other data may be cached), and the full global city database isn't distributable for the same reason; several headline products are China-only (minutely precipitation, tropical cyclone, most indices); warning and air-quality coverage vary by country and need checking against their own coverage pages; the free allotment covers only the first price group, so tides, storms, and especially solar radiation (¥0.3/request from the first call) bill immediately; pricing is CNY-only and the sole online payment method is Alipay (bank transfer for organization accounts), which is a practical barrier for developers outside China; QPM isn't published publicly; bulk data retrieval requires contacting a business manager
- **Built on:** QWeather describes its service as based on meteorological and AI algorithms, and its terms reference third-party data sources requiring attribution, but the underlying NWP models and providers are not publicly enumerated
