### Tempest (WeatherFlow-Tempest)

Weather API built around a consumer hardware network — WeatherFlow-Tempest sells the Tempest home weather station and exposes everything it measures, plus derived metrics, statistics, lightning, and a 10-day "Better Forecast," through REST and WebSocket endpoints. Unusually for this catalog, free API access is gated on owning a station rather than on a request quota, and it is strictly non-commercial: anything beyond personal use requires a TempestONE subscription.

- **Based in:** Santa Cruz, California, USA (operating base; WeatherFlow-Tempest, Inc. is registered at 921 S Clyde Morris Blvd, Daytona Beach, Florida, and the corporate phone carries a Santa Cruz area code — note both locations come from third-party company registries rather than the company's own site, which doesn't publish an address)
- **Access model:** Mixed, and hardware-anchored — **personal access is instant self-serve but requires owning a Tempest station**: sign in to the Tempest web app and generate a Personal Access Token, or use OAuth 2.0 for apps with a web interface. The interactive documentation can be explored without owning hardware, but tokens only return data for stations the authenticated user owns. **Commercial access requires a TempestONE subscription or a specific commercial agreement, arranged through sales contact** (`business.tempest.earth/contact-professional-services`)
- **Pricing model:** Custom / contact for pricing (data access for station owners is bundled into the hardware purchase with no ongoing fee; TempestONE commercial subscriptions are quoted per use case with no published figures — the TempestOne Weather System hardware is listed at $649 USD, but the subscription tiers are not priced publicly)
- **Coverage / data types:** two API families. **Tempest Home API** (personal) — station metadata, current conditions and observations (all measured and derived values, including sea-level pressure, daily rain accumulation, and wet-bulb globe temperature), daily/weekly/monthly/annual/all-time statistics, and the Better Forecast 10-day forecast for your station. **TempestOne API** (commercial) — the same plus network-wide station metadata, observations, diagnostics, and lightning strike data (location, type, strength, timestamp). **TempestOne Extended API** adds forecasts for any point on Earth, radar and satellite imagery including global sea surface temperatures, and sea surface currents and tide data for select regions. The observation network covers Tempest stations plus 100,000+ other surface stations from public and private networks. A local UDP broadcast interface is also documented for off-grid use
- **Free tier:** no request-quota free tier in the usual sense — instead, station owners get unlimited **personal, non-commercial** access to their own stations' observations, forecasts, metadata and statistics at no ongoing cost, under "rate/volume limits (enough for personal use)" that are **not published numerically**. Any user can read public metadata from public stations; observation and forecast data from stations you don't own is unavailable without a TempestONE subscription, and data from private stations is unavailable entirely
- **Paid tiers:** TempestONE subscription — custom / contact for pricing, described as "tailored... and priced to meet your exact needs," with an Enterprise tier for bulk deployments and custom solutions. Sector-specific packages exist for utilities, coastal, emergency management, schools, and heat safety
- **Rate limits:** not published — the access policy states only that personal use carries "lower" application-level rate and volume limits and commercial agreements carry "higher" ones, with no figures given at either level
- **Output formats:** JSON over REST; real-time streaming over WebSocket (`wss://ws.weatherflow.com`), delivering a new observation roughly every minute; local UDP broadcast on the station's own network as a documented fallback
- **Geographic strength:** the observation network is densest where Tempest hardware has sold — heavily North America, with the wider 100,000+ station network drawn from public and private sources. Forecasts for arbitrary global coordinates are a TempestOne Extended feature; the free station-owner forecast endpoint is anchored to a station you own
- **Notable limitations:** **the free tier requires buying hardware** — there is no coordinate-based free forecast, and the personal `better_forecast` endpoint takes a `station_id` parameter with no latitude/longitude option, so this is not a drop-in replacement for a general-purpose forecast API; personal use is **strictly non-commercial**, defined broadly enough to exclude academic and organisational use, and rebroadcast "may be limited" even for personal users; no observation or forecast data is available from private stations under any agreement, and public-station data needs a TempestONE subscription; commercial pricing isn't published at any tier; rate and volume limits aren't published at either tier; the underlying data is a consumer-grade sensor network, so station siting quality varies in a way it doesn't for official met-office observations; the access policy is explicitly "subject to change"
- **Built on:** WeatherFlow-Tempest's proprietary **Nearcast AI™** — the company's own quality-control, augmentation and short-range forecasting layer over its Tempest station network, supplemented by 100,000+ third-party surface stations and, per the company's own description, "dozens of other forecast models from NOAA and other government sources." The specific NWP models in the blend are not enumerated
